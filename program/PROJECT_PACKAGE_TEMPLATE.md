# Full Science Project Package Template

## A. Project identity
- Topic ID and title
- Useful-result ledger IDs
- One-sentence discovery
- Status: exploratory / validated / transported / closed negative / graduated
- New direction and why it matters
- What a top-lab reviewer would ask next

## B. Approximately 20-page report blueprint
1. Cover and project identity
2. Structured abstract
3. The surprising question
4. Literature landscape and explicit gap
5. Novelty and falsifiable hypotheses
6. Open datasets, rights and provenance
7. Locked protocol and endpoints
8. Methods and computational environment
9. Statistical design and sampling unit
10. Primary results
11. External/transport validation
12. Negative controls and ablations
13. Robustness, confounders and failure modes
14. Biological or mechanistic interpretation
15. Contradictions and honest negative evidence
16. Working tool or application workflow
17. Real user, decision and alternatives
18. Safety, ethics, privacy and misuse
19. Limits and next experiments
20. Conclusions, references and artifact links
Appendices: full tables, source ledger, checksums, commands and supplement.

## C. Research paper
- Title, abstract, introduction, related work
- Methods with frozen decisions distinguished from post-outcome diagnostics
- Results with effect sizes and uncertainty
- Discussion, limits and future work
- Data/code availability, contributions, conflicts and references
- Supplement with protocols and full diagnostics

## D. Working tool/application acceptance tests
- Real user and decision named
- Inputs/outputs and claim boundary documented
- Reproducible build and pinned environment
- Unit/integration tests and real-data smoke test
- Baseline/alternative comparison
- Calibration or abstention where needed
- Failure messages, monitoring and rollback
- License/data-rights/security/privacy review
- No placeholder screens or synthetic substitutes

## E. External-review kit
- One-page abstract
- Poster/figure set with readable legends
- Five-minute and fifteen-minute explanations
- Expert question bank: novelty, controls, statistics, mechanism, limits, independence, impact and next work
- Reproduction checklist

## F. Delivery
- Drive report, paper, tool artifact/data and checksums
- Science-program repository commit and user-usable links
- Useful-result count updated
