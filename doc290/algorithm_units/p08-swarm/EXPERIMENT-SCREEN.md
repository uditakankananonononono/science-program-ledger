# Initial swarm-delivery evidence screen

Focused endpoint/admission screen, not a complete literature survey or absence claim.
Fetched primary article:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7909881/
"Ultrasound Doppler-guided real-time navigation of a magnetic microswarm for active
endovascular delivery" (2021). No article/supplement data imported into computation.

Material endpoint distinctions: Table 1 labels percentages as swarm area ratio
S_flow/S_stagnant. These are NOT per-agent Bernoulli arrival probabilities and must
not be fed into our payload model as p. The discussion reports nanoparticle access
rate after roughly 20-mm navigation and aggregate residual/recycled nanoparticles,
not an identified per-agent joint arrival table. Figure 4 describes repeated
experiments/standard deviations; these are not proof of equal-payload single-robot
versus N-agent budgets. The paper describes strong particle interactions enabling
collective navigation; it does not supply validated independence for our model.
Ex vivo porcine coronary navigation is a distinct experimental domain, not in-vivo
universal physiology or direct calibration of synthetic outcome probabilities.

PMC license information states CC BY-NC 4.0. Open reading is not unrestricted dataset
admission; supplement/raw data rights and exact bytes not qualified. No calibration
value admitted. The bounded fetched sections did not establish complete per-agent
joint delivery/collateral records, nor equal energy/information budgets. This is not
proof those records cannot be obtained elsewhere or by contacting authors.
