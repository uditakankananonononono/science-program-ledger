## Boundary analysis
Unconditional masked-LM sampling produces generic protein-like peptides that essentially never land in AMP space (0.2% vs 64% for real AMPs), while shuffled AMP compositions score 51x higher (10.2%). Together these say AMP-likeness (as measured by an independent published predictor) lives in residue ORDER - amphipathic patterning - not in composition, and an unconditional 8M PLM does not sample AMP-typical order at usable rates. This is the generative-direction confirmation of the 011/012 boundary: small PLMs are informative scorers/embedders but weak unconditional designers. Any future AMP design experiment needs CONDITIONING (AMP-prompted or fine-tuned generator - fine-tuning outside current compute) or a search/guidance loop (score-guided iterative refinement) - each a fresh hypothesis with its own locked gates, not an arm of this one.

## Instrument + errata notes
Macrel default output is POSITIVES-ONLY (is_AMP filter); --keep-negatives required for full-set probabilities. Initial scoring pass read the positives-only file as full output; caught and re-scored with the correct flag before any gate verdict (same class of fix as the METENG percentile calibration). Macrel also strips leading Met and drops >100aa/duplicates (filter_smorfs). The 0.50 threshold saturates on composition-matched shuffles (calibration: 100%/100% at <=0.5 scale) - the locked 0.80 threshold is the discriminative one.
