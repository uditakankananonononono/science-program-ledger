Universal Confounder Stress Test for Bio-AI

A preregistered, three-domain evaluation of whether a model-agnostic audit score can detect validation optimism.

Locked outcome: negative

The composite did not satisfy the all-required success gate. Leave-one-domain-out sensitivity was 0.571, and two leave-one-dimension-out variants outperformed the full score. A stricter protocol-compliance audit also found that four nominal calculator passes were only partially implemented and therefore cannot be treated as demonstrated.

Round completed: 21 September 2026

Open-data computational research. No wet-lab work. This report is a negative-result technical dossier, not a success paper or a validated medical-device claim.

Executive decision

The Round 1 question was whether one fixed audit score, built from model-agnostic diagnostics, could identify configurations whose naive validation overstated rigorous generalization across clinical tabular data, multi-study transcriptomics, and longitudinal digital biomarkers. The answer is not yet. The score correlated with measured optimism (Spearman ρ 0.770), and material optimism appeared in breast pCR and Parkinson telemonitoring. However, correlation did not translate into a stable universal detector. Cross-domain sensitivity missed the locked 0.80 threshold, and removing prevalence or calibration improved AUROC.

Operational decision: Do not use this score as an automated release gate, certification tool, or safety claim. It can be retained as an exploratory evidence dossier that forces teams to inspect split design, unit overlap, cohort shift, calibration, and subgroup instability. Human review remains mandatory.

What is solid

  • Real open datasets were used, with 99,343 eligible diabetes encounters, five breast cancer studies totaling 741 endpoint-labeled samples, and 5,875 longitudinal Parkinson observations from 42 subjects.
  • Naive and group/study-aware evaluations were executed with train-only preprocessing.
  • The direction and magnitude of optimism were large for Parkinson models and moderate for pooled breast transcriptomics, while diabetes readmission showed little split-induced inflation.
  • The frozen protocol, code, processed result tables, diagnostics, environment, and checksums are preserved.

What is not established

  • A transportable threshold that detects material optimism in a new biomedical domain.
  • Incremental value from all six implemented score dimensions.
  • All inferential and negative-control details specified in the locked protocol.
  • End-to-end reproducibility from source archives in a clean environment.
