# Sculpted Project Summary - The Small Protein Problem

## Status
Multi-round mixed/negative project parked after external data-transport failure. No universal short-protein deficit claim graduates.

## Cumulative useful result
Initial bacterial and mammalian analyses suggested evidence gaps for short proteins. Successive rounds showed why that headline was too broad:
- contradiction resolution preserved a bacterial gap but reversed the apparent PDB advantage after balancing; mammal/yeast gaps collapsed;
- within 82 shared Pfam families, the bacterial whole-cohort gap vanished;
- eukaryotic all-Pfam/MMseqs designs were non-estimable under support floors;
- PRIDE metadata could not establish peptide/inference/species/support gates;
- a frozen six-project harmonization manifest was built, but PRIDE HTTPS TLS EOF and FTP timeout were independently reproduced.

The durable finding is that broad "small proteins are ignored" estimates are dominated by family composition, taxonomy, opportunity and evidence-source accessibility. Proteomics confirmation remains unestimated, not negative.

## What is new
The project repeatedly stress-tested a compelling representation-bias story against family matching, taxa, structure, annotation opportunity and proteomics evidence. It maps where the question becomes non-estimable rather than forcing a universal conclusion.

## Why it matters
Small-protein discovery claims can confuse biology with annotation and database opportunity. This project provides a design/audit framework for asking the question without that confounding.

## Working tool/application
A small-protein evidence-gap auditor can stratify by family, taxon, annotation age, structure opportunity and proteomics support; calculate estimability/support floors; and emit explicit non-estimability rather than a biased score. The frozen PRIDE manifest can be resumed unchanged on a host with sanctioned transport.

## Top-lab reviewer questions
1. Does the gap reappear under a prospectively frozen, peptide-level PRIDE extraction with species and protein-inference controls?
2. Can long/short proteins be matched within families on detectability, abundance and publication opportunity?
3. Which taxa and evidence types retain a deficit after these controls?
4. Are novel short proteins missed by gene calling, by experiments, or only by annotation systems?

## Next direction
Resume the exact harmonization manifest on infrastructure with reliable PRIDE FTP/Aspera or a sanctioned mirror. Do not reinterpret transport failure as absent proteomic evidence.
