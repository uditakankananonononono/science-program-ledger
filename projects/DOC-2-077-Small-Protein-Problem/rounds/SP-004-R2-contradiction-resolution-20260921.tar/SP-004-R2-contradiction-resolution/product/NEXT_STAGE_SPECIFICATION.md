# Domain-aware small-protein evidence audit

User: database curator or small-protein experimental group. Output: reason-coded evidence gaps by function comment, experimental GO, PDB and AlphaFold, always stratified by taxon and family. Forbidden: combining endpoints into a biological "unknownness" score or inferring function.

Prospective trial: within domain/family and a 90-110 aa bandwidth, randomize curator review to reason-coded ranking or random order. Primary endpoint is actionable evidence updates per hour; report false-priority rate, inter-curator agreement, taxon equity and redundant review. Require >=25% yield improvement with confidence interval above zero, no major taxon receiving under half its eligible proportional allocation, and human approval before any database edit.
