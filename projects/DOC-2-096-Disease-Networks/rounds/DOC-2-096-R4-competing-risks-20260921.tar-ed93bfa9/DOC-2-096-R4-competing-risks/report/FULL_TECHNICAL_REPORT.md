# DOC-2-096 R4: Competing-Risk-Set Redesign

**Status:** PREDICTIVE TRIAGE CLOSED; DESCRIPTIVE TOPOLOGY RETAINED

## Result
Separating risk sets fixed R3's eligibility artifact and revealed stable simple signals:

- Conflict emergence among clean edges: raw variant burden transported from 2.02x development to 2.22x replication top-decile enrichment, with precision 67.2% versus 30.3% prevalence.
- Resolution among conflicted edges: *lower* raw variant burden transported from 2.91x to 2.69x, precision 15.1% versus 5.6%.
- Review upgrade among non-expert edges: pathogenic count transported from 1.68x to 2.37x, precision 7.36% versus 3.10%.

These are useful mechanism findings: testing/variant burden drives conflict emergence, sparse conflicted edges resolve more often, and pathogenic evidence burden precedes review upgrades.

## Why the predictive gate still failed
The locked product gate required an outcome-specific model to beat the best simple baseline by 20% with block uncertainty. The strongest scores *were the simple baselines themselves*. Reliability and benign burden were consistently weaker or unstable. No more complex frozen model had preregistered justification or evidence of incremental value. Rather than promote raw burden as a novel predictor, R4 abstains and closes predictive triage.

## Bounded contribution
A transparent operational queue could sort by variant burden for human reviewers, but this is workflow prioritization based on database activity, not disease risk, pathogenicity or calibrated probability. It likely reflects ascertainment and testing volume. The publishable contribution remains R0's descriptive negative-evidence topology plus R4's outcome-specific mechanism decomposition.

## Cross-round synthesis
R0 showed benign/conflict context rewires disease networks. R1 blocked snapshot-date leakage. R2 showed reliability probabilities do not transport. R3 showed a combined-outcome ranker fails. R4 shows why: different events have different risk sets and simple burden mechanisms. Since no added negative-evidence model beats simple counts, the predictive product is closed.
