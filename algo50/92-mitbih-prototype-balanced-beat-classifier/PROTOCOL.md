# P6 preregistration (locked before any DS2 evaluation)
Data: MIT-BIH Arrhythmia DB 1.0.0 (ODC-BY 1.0), 144 dat/hea/atr files verified against official SHA256SUMS.txt (SHA256SUMS.txt sha256 b61158a96d5f2ca80edfb354a9a66a6324836c390a84e1966dcee2b907d6be43). Channel 0, 360 Hz.
Protocol: de Chazal inter-patient split. DS1 (train) = 101,106,108,109,112,114,115,116,118,119,122,124,201,203,205,207,208,209,215,220,223,230. DS2 (test) = 100,103,105,111,113,117,121,123,200,202,210,212,213,214,219,221,222,228,231,232,233,234. Paced records 102,104,107,217 excluded.
Beats: annotated beat locations; AAMI classes N={N,L,R,e,j}, S={A,a,J,S}, V={V,E}, F={F}; Q/unknown excluded. Window = 90 samples before to 144 after the annotation, resampled to 90 points by linear interpolation, each beat z-normalised; plus 3 RR features (pre-RR, post-RR, local mean of previous 10 RR), scaled by the DS1 mean/std. Features computed identically for all arms.
Baselines (hyperparameters chosen on DS1 by 5-fold GROUP CV by record, macro-F1 over S,V,F): B1 kNN (k in {5,15,45}); B2 L2 logistic regression (C in {0.1,1,10}, class_weight=balanced). Baseline used for the headline comparison = the one with higher DS1 group-CV score.
New method PBC (prototype-balanced classifier): StandardScaler + PCA(20); per-class k-means prototypes with m_c = min(m, class size) where m in {4,8,16} (random_state 0, n_init 5); score_c(x)= -min distance to class c prototypes; decision = argmax of score_c + b_c where biases b_c are fit on DS1 by coordinate search over a grid {-1..+1 step 0.25} (class N fixed 0) maximizing group-CV macro-F1 over S,V,F (the same 5 group folds); m chosen by the same CV.
Primary metric: DS2 macro-F1 over S,V,F pooled over beats. Paired bootstrap over DS2 RECORDS (10000, seed 7) of F1(PBC) - F1(best baseline).
WIN: diff >= +0.02 and CI lower > 0. NEGATIVE: CI upper < 0. Else NULL. Verbatim, no re-banding. Secondary: per-class F1, accuracy. DS2 touched once.
Grid edges declared: m in {4,8,16} (edge 4 and 16), biases in [-1,1]; if the CV optimum lands on an edge it is reported as such and not extended after the fact.
