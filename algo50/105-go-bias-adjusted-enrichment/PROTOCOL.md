THIS IS A SIMULATION STUDY on real annotations: the planted signal and the confounder are simulated; there are no real study gene lists with known truth. Conclusions are about calibration/power under a stated bias model, not about real experiments.
Annotation table: GAF rows with qualifier not containing NOT, any evidence code except ND, UniProt accession as gene; propagate along is_a and part_of (non-obsolete terms) to ancestors. Universe = genes with >=1 annotation (n_annot_g = number of propagated terms of gene g). Testable terms: 10 <= |genes| <= 500.
Generation: a replicate picks a planted term T (|genes|, 20..150), a list of 100 genes = 8 genes drawn uniformly from T plus 92 background genes drawn WITHOUT replacement from the universe minus T with probability proportional to n_annot_g^1 (annotation-richness bias, the confounder; secondary misspecified generator: weight n_annot^0.5). Related set R(T) = T, its ancestors and descendants (within testable terms), and testable terms with Jaccard(gene set) >= 0.3 to T.
Methods (all over testable terms with the list's overlap k>=1, p=1 otherwise; BH over ALL testable terms; call = BH q < 0.05): 
 B Fisher exact (hypergeometric sf), published baseline.
 NEW BIAS-ADJ: Fisher's noncentral hypergeometric (scipy nchypergeom_fisher) with odds = (mean n_annot of genes in term / mean n_annot of universe genes not in term)^gamma, gamma tuned on DEV in {0.5,1,1.5} (edges 0.5 and 1.5 declared). Uses the true form of the generator's bias, so the primary test is an in-model correction; the misspecified generator is the robustness check.
Splits: testable T terms sorted by id; DEV T = even positions (100 replicates, seed 31); TEST T = odd positions (300 replicates, seed 32), run once. Disjoint terms.
Metrics per replicate: FP = number of called terms not in R(T); REC = 1 if T itself is called. Primary criterion (both required): (a) mean FP of BIAS-ADJ <= 0.75 x mean FP of Fisher AND bootstrap 95% CI (replicates, 10000, seed 7) of the paired FP difference (Fisher minus ADJ) lower bound > 0; (b) mean REC of ADJ >= mean REC of Fisher minus 0.05. WIN = (a) and (b). NEGATIVE = (a) fails with the CI upper < 0 of the FP difference OR (b) fails by more than 0.05 while FP drops (cost reported). Else NULL. Misspecified-generator result is secondary, no verdict. gamma chosen on DEV by minimum FP subject to DEV REC >= Fisher DEV REC - 0.05; if none qualifies use gamma=1. No re-banding.
Equivalence check: Fisher implementation vs a direct 2x2 scipy.stats.fisher_exact on 50 random (k,K,n,N) cells (<1e-9), and ADJ with odds=1 equals Fisher.
