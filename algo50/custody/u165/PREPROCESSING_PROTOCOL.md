# Unit 165 fatigue13 - Stage A preprocessing-only protocol

Source: Zenodo record 14182446 v2, https://zenodo.org/records/14182446 . Dataset license CC BY 4.0 from live exact-record API. Whole raw sEMG_data.zip MD5 ea7338b95543ada98b1ddc65d4b6aabc verified over 3299567337 bytes, ordered hash-only stream. Nested DEV subject ZIPs must pass publisher-verified archive provenance, outer member CRC, independent local SHA256, and inner member CRC before parsing.

Split: natural-numeric sorted subject IDs 1 through 13, positions 0-based. Even positions DEV 1,3,5,7,9,11,13; odd positions TEST 2,4,6,8,10,12. Subject split precedes all extraction/analysis. TEST payload is not parsed, surveyed, fitted or scored. This rule later applies mechanically to TEST only after authorization and lock, never to refine cleaning.

Cleaning rule: remove a maximal trailing all-eight-column-zero suffix only if its first row decreases time from the preceding row; zero rows without such a reset are KEPT; any remaining nonmonotonic timestamp, multiple resets, an entirely-zero trial, or an absent valid pre-reset prefix flags the WHOLE TRIAL unusable; no interpolation, no mid-trial deletion.

Operational definitions: trial CSVs have four pairs of timestamp and EMG columns, eight columns total. All-zero means exact numeric zero in every column. Time decrease means at least one timestamp column strictly decreases; nonmonotonic means a timestamp step is less than or equal to zero. The maximal suffix excludes no earlier row. A valid pre-reset prefix is nonempty, numeric, finite, with strictly increasing timestamps after the initial row. Entirely-zero trial means all signal channels are zero for every retained row. Multiple resets means at least two distinct row boundaries with a time decrease before cleaning. Nonnumeric/nonfinite or unexpected-schema trials are flagged unusable and counted, not repaired.

Flagged-trial disposition: exclude by this frozen rule with exact counts reported. If exclusions exceed 10% of DEV trials overall, or any DEV subject loses more than half its 12 trials, stop and report before locking Stage B. Removal of terminal suffix rows alone is not a whole-trial exclusion. Report separately subjects/trials affected, exact rows removed per trial, whole-trial exclusions and reasons, and timestamp sampling/range checks.

Clock joins: source paper documents hardware-trigger start synchronization and longer self-report stop times. Survey actual DEV timestamp starts, strictly increasing retained sensor clocks and label clocks, four-clock agreement, sampling interval and overlap. Do not invent shift/rescaling or fatigue labels. Labels remain native self-perceived 0/1/2, not clinical truth. Modeling uses only verified common time support; a final label/window policy belongs to Stage B after survey.

Rationale: an all-column-zero terminal row after a time reset is a padding artifact, not physiology. Earlier exploration already inspected DEV subject 1 and found one terminal zero row each in trials 4,6,10,12; the frozen rule and thresholds are not claimed blind to that inspection. No TEST was surveyed.

Stage A scope: parsing, deterministic DEV survey and descriptive admission checks only; no headroom, power estimates, fitting, outcome compute, prereg lock or pushes. After Stage A hash verification and survey, Stage B DEV feasibility/headroom/power gates use this frozen preprocessing. WIN delta 0.03 and 80% power gate are unchanged. Full modeling prereg is drafted only after those gates pass and sent for review/lock. No outcome compute on TEST.
