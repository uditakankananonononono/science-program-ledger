DATA SCOPE: IntAct (not BioGRID) human MITAB, release Jan14 2026, CC BY 4.0 (IMEx policy), scout-verified full archive sha256 4464f68a9b405bb5fddaced6e0a6b1b8e4386b65956c41cb22763c81d5337a46 (1,171,103,835 B); I use the scout's COMPACT PROJECTION only: intact-human-physical.compact.tsv.gz, 7,819,264 B, sha256 7e9f6bba8ea0511e510b71a383817dc22b32469e799bb3457c769e331d442a85 (436,299 evidence rows: both taxid 9606, both protein, physical MI:0915 or direct MI:0407, Negative=false). I did not re-derive it from the 1.2 GB archive (not feasible on this box). Columns kept: IDs A/B, detection method, publication IDs, taxids, interaction type, interaction IDs, confidence, creation/update date, negative flag.
Graph: nodes = UniProt accessions (prefix uniprotkb:, isoform/chain suffix after '-' removed, self-pairs dropped); an undirected edge = a distinct accession pair; edge time = the MINIMUM creation date over its evidence rows. Creation date is a CURATION date, not publication date; bulk screens make the curation timeline lumpy (2016 and 2025 hold large batches), so test positives are not independent; disclosed.
Splits: DEV: train graph = edges first curated <=2010-12-31; DEV test = edges first curated 2011-01-01..2013-12-31 with both endpoints in the train graph. FINAL: train graph = edges first curated <=2015-12-31; test = edges first curated 2016-01-01..2021-12-31 with both endpoints in the train graph. In each, cap positives at 10,000 by random sample (seed 3). Negatives: 10,000 pairs, each endpoint drawn uniformly from the multiset of endpoints of the positive pairs (degree-biased), requiring the pair is not an edge in the FULL graph (any date) and not a self pair (seed 4). FINAL touched once.
Scores (computed on the train graph only): published baselines: CN (common neighbours), AA (Adamic-Adar), RA (resource allocation), L3 (Kovacs et al. 2019: sum over length-3 paths u-a-b-v of 1/sqrt(k_a k_b)). Headline baseline = best DEV AUROC among AA, RA, L3 (CN reported, excluded from headline selection as dominated by AA/RA).
New method DCD (degree-corrected diffusion): A_n = D^-alpha A D^-alpha (alpha in {0.5,0.75,1}); score(u,v) = (A_n^2)_uv + w3 (A_n^3)_uv + w4 (A_n^4)_uv, walk counts, w3,w4 in {0,0.5,1}; 27 configs tuned on DEV by AUROC (ties -> smaller alpha, then smaller w3, w4). Grid edges: alpha 0.5/1, w 0/1 declared; edge selection reported. Note DCD overlaps L3 (alpha=0.5,w2=0,w4=0 is L3-like walk variant); L3 is therefore in the baseline set.
Metric: AUROC with average-rank tie handling (scipy rankdata; zero-score ties get 0.5). Secondary: average precision. Paired bootstrap over the 20,000 labelled pairs (2000 resamples, seed 7) of AUROC difference (DCD minus headline baseline). WIN: AUROC diff >= +0.02 and CI lower > 0. NEGATIVE: CI upper < 0. Else NULL. No re-banding.
Equivalence check: on a tiny 6-node graph with hand-computed values, CN/AA/RA/L3 implementations must match the hand values; run.py asserts it before any scoring.
