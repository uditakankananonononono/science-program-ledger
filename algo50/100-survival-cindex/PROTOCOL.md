# P100 preregistration (locked before any survival model evaluation)
Data: CRAN survival 3.8-12 source archive (sha256 ebb70d9a4e7e8ad7f886a563184d6f079e64c0591a0bce219ae90967f0bdf994, matches the scout's), License LGPL (>=2) per its DESCRIPTION (not GPL). Datasets pbc (Mayo PBC, 418 patients) and lung (NCCTG, 228) decoded with pyreadr; neither was scored before this lock. Scout scope: sources verified, R objects not decoded or scored.
Primary data: PBC rows with complete Mayo-model covariates (age, edema, bili, albumin, protime). Event = status==2 (death); transplant (status 1) and alive (0) censored. Covariates: age, edema, log(bili), log(albumin), log(protime) (no standardisation needed; penalised Cox).
Splits: 20 repeated stratified (by event) 70/30 splits, seeds 1..20 (StratifiedShuffleSplit). Overlapping, so not independent replications (disclosed). Test touched once per split.
Baseline B: lifelines 0.30.x CoxPHFitter(penalizer=0.1, l1_ratio=0), fitted on the split's training part; risk score = -S(t=4 years=1461 days | x) (the same horizon score for all arms).
New method PWC (piecewise-coefficient Cox): break time tau = 1096 days (3 years), fixed before any run. Early model: Cox on training data administratively censored at tau. Late model: Cox on training patients with time > tau, delayed entry at tau (entry_col), same penalizer. Predicted S(t|x) = S_early(tau|x) * S_late(t|x)/S_late(tau|x) for t=1461; if the late model cannot be fitted (<10 late events) PWC falls back to B for that split (counted and reported).
Metric (primary): Harrell C-index of the horizon risk score on the test part; mean over splits; paired difference PWC-B; 95% bootstrap CI over splits (10000, seed 7). WIN: diff >= +0.01 and CI lower > 0. NEGATIVE: CI upper < 0. Else NULL. Secondary, same rules, no verdict weight: LUNG (NCCTG, covariates age, sex, ph.ecog, ph.karno, wt.loss complete cases; event = status==2; horizon 1 year=365 days, tau = 180 days).
Equivalence check: PWC with tau=infinity must reproduce B's risk scores exactly (asserted on split 1).
Disclosure: small events counts, overlapping splits, no external cohort; transplant-censoring informative; tau fixed not tuned.
