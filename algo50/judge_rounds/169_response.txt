Disposition supported, disclosure mostly adequate. A BUILD-A DROP pre-lock is reasonable: the proposed composite is substantially worse than the strongest standalone baseline on DEV (AUROC 0.55 vs 0.79), and the bootstrap CI excludes zero. But this is only exploratory evidence from 20 DEV subjects, with no preregistration, lock, or TEST evaluation. Calling it a “negative” study result would be inappropriate. The archive/container-granularity caveat is useful, but the absence of a permutation run limits the auditability of the observed comparison.

Biggest methodological weakness: small, exploratory DEV sample driving a complex model-selection comparison. With only 20 subjects, the −0.24 estimate and its bootstrap interval can be unstable, while comparing multiple utterance constructions/model candidates creates selection pressure. Subject-level CV prevents the obvious subject leakage, but does not make the estimate independent of exploratory candidate selection.

Stronger design: preregister the candidate set and analysis before opening labels/numerics, then use nested subject-level CV entirely within DEV for selection, followed by a genuinely untouched TEST cohort/partition for one locked comparison against the prespecified strongest baseline. Ideally repeat across independent datasets or subject splits rather than treating one 20-subject DEV result as decisive.
