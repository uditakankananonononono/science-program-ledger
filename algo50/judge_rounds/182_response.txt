Label supported and honest, with one caveat. “Positive DEV signal, underpowered for a confirmatory claim” is defensible. The observed subject-level MAE reduction is 1.42° versus a 0.5° preregistered threshold, the paired bootstrap CI excludes zero, and 23/31 subjects improve. Calling it confirmatory would be inappropriate because there is no untouched TEST set and the preregistered power at the target effect was only 0.199. The 0.883 post-hoc power at the observed effect should not be used as evidence of adequate prospective power.

Biggest methodological weakness: the MLP did not reliably converge. Four of five outer folds reached the 250-iteration cap with ConvergenceWarning. Thus the reported model is a computationally truncated fit, making the comparison potentially dependent on an arbitrary optimization ceiling. This is more consequential than the modest sample size alone. Also, a single grouped 5-fold CV is an internal DEV estimate, not independent confirmation.

Stronger design: predefine a genuinely untouched subject-level TEST cohort and use the remaining subjects for nested, convergence-controlled development. Within training data, tune architecture/regularization and establish a sufficiently high or convergence-based optimization criterion without inspecting TEST subjects. Then evaluate baseline versus MLP once on the locked TEST subjects, reporting paired subject-level MAE differences and a confidence interval. If 31 subjects cannot support that split adequately, recruit/add independent subjects rather than treating repeated CV as confirmation.
