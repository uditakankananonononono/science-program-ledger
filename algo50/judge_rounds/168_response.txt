Disposition supported; disclosure mostly adequate. Calling this a DEV-only win, not confirmatory is appropriate because no TEST member was opened. The locked rerun and explicit distinction between real (perm=false) and permutation runs are good. However, “power 0.944” is only model-based/conditional on the observed DEV effect and does not establish adequate power for an unseen TEST population. The best-of-4 baseline and best-of-2 new-method selection are also post-comparison selection and make the reported +0.1369 optimistic; the disclosure acknowledges this but does not quantify it.

Biggest methodological weakness: model/variant selection on the same DEV data used for the claimed effect. Choosing the strongest baseline and strongest N configuration using DEV CV, then reporting their paired delta and CI, creates selection-induced optimism. The seed sensitivity analysis does not solve this because seeds are not independent datasets and the locked CI applies only to one selected setting.

Stronger design: use nested grouped cross-validation with identity components as the grouping unit: an inner CV exclusively for choosing baseline/model/features/segment handling, and an untouched outer DEV fold for estimating the effect. Freeze the entire pipeline after this, then evaluate once on a genuinely unopened TEST cohort/member. Report the outer-fold paired AUROC distribution and a pre-specified paired test/CI. If multiple datasets/members exist, reserve one entire member for TEST rather than selecting segments/records across members.
